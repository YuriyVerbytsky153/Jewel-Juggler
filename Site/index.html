A juggling act for the whole court

Jewel Juggler is a one-thumb arcade game with real depth: every catch, toss and bank is a small decision.

Easy to pick up

Drag anywhere to slide the jester. Gems you catch fly back up by themselves, so you can focus on timing.

Hard to master

Gems crack with every toss. Bank them late for bigger rewards, but never past the breaking point.

Fifteen acts

Staged challenges with goals and roses, an endless Royal Performance and a fresh Festival Night every day.

How it works

Three moves, one stage, and a cap full of bells.

Catch

Slide under falling gems. Catch them dead centre to ring the cap and charge the Jingle, which slows every gem for three seconds.

Toss

Caught gems arc back into the air. The more gems you keep aloft at once, the louder the applause, until the court calls an Encore.

Bank

Tap the stage and the next catch flies into the chest. Only banked gems pay ducats for new props and costumes.

Signature mechanics

Invented for this court, and all of them work together.

Chord Fusion

Catch three gems of the same colour in a row and the third fuses into a Crown Gem. It falls faster, starts with no cracks and banks for five times the points.

Wear & Bank

Every toss deepens the cracks. A gem glowing red cannot take another toss: bank it now or it shatters in your hands and takes a bell.

Applause & Encore

Keep many gems in the air and the crowd rises to its feet. A full house calls an Encore: double points, and the king throws in one more gem.

Royal Decrees

The king announces his whims a few seconds ahead: Ruby Hour, Rotten Tomatoes that must be let fall, or a Candle Gust that darkens the hall.

The Joker Gem

A rare harlequin gem that completes any chord. Handle it with care: dropping it costs two bells.

The Jingle

Sweet-spot catches ring the cap. When it is fully charged, one press slows every gem in the hall.

Support

Questions, ideas or a bug to report? Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will answer within a few working days.

What are ducats? Can I buy them?

Ducats are the court's play currency. You earn them only by banking gems and winning roses. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything outside the game.

Why did my gem shatter?

Each toss cracks a gem a little more. When its cracks glow red, the next catch must be banked (tap the stage or press BANK first), otherwise it shatters and costs a bell.

How do I earn three roses in an act?

Finish the act's goal without losing a single bell. Losing one bell gives two roses, more gives one. Each new rose also pays 40 ducats.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Jewel Juggler plays completely offline and keeps your progress on your device.

Is there a left-handed layout?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Left-handed to swap the BANK and JINGLE buttons. Sound, music and haptics can be switched there too.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress erases ducats, props, costumes, roses and posters. Your settings stay as they are.
